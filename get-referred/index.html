WHY GET REFERRED WITH US

Get seen by people who work where you want to work

🎓

Referred by IIM alumni leaders

Your profile goes to IIM alumni and senior and mid-level professionals who currently work at the company and are active in the industry.

🏢

Referred on the company's own portal

Your referral is submitted through the company's internal referral system, so your application is flagged as an employee referral.

🎯

One verified contact, one-to-one

Each request goes to a verified referral POC at your target company, not to a mass mailing list.

✍️

A recommendation that speaks for you

Your resume travels with a short note of recommendation, so your referrer can vouch for you quickly.

📊

Track every step

Get an email at each step and follow your request in Status of Referrals, from submitted to referred.

🔒

Private by design

Only our team and your referral contact see your details, and you can ask us to delete them at any time.

HOW IT WORKS

From your resume to a referral in five steps

  1. 1

    Upload your resume

    PDF or Word, up to 2 MB.

  2. 2

    Choose the company and job

    Pick your target company, open its careers site and copy the job ID.

  3. 3

    Share your details

    Add your contact details and a short note of recommendation. They come straight to My Career Company, and we email you a confirmation.

  4. 4

    We connect you to a referral POC

    Our team shares your profile with a verified POC at the company and emails you when it's done.

  5. 5

    The POC refers you

    The POC submits your referral on the company's portal. Confirm it in Status of Referrals when the company emails you.

Referral contacts decide whether to refer each candidate, and a referral doesn't guarantee an interview or an offer.
